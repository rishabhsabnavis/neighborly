# Brief for Designer 2: story, copy, and pitch

No code. Everything here goes into a shared doc (Google Doc or Claude Doc). The developers copy it into the product. Neighborly in one line: after Hurricane Isaias hits the Florida panhandle, an AI agent texts Neighborly policyholders, gets them help by text, voice or a human, and turns what they said into a claim draft. Read `docs/SPEC.md` (the demo script) and the Figma brief (https://claude.ai/artifact/7bZErNhK8kDqRttxuDaK4S) first.

## Tonight (by ~10pm Saturday)
1. **Message copy (to the owner).** Every text the agent sends: the check-in menu, the reply for each option (1 Safe, 2 Need help, 3 Emergency, 4 Call me, 0 Person), the policy-based follow-up questions, the claim-started text, and the JOIN confirmation. Rules: under 300 characters, calm, plain words, no jargon. Option 3 must say "call 911".
2. **Voice agent personality and lines (to the owner).** How the voice agent greets, what it already knows when it calls ("I saw your text about the roof"), the 3-4 questions it asks, how it hands off to a person, and how it ends the call. Warm, short sentences.
3. **Seed stories (to Dev B).** About 30 short, realistic situations across the panhandle counties: what each person texted, what they said on a call, and how urgent it was. Include Maria Lopez (Bay County, roof caved in, mother on home oxygen) exactly as in the spec. Mix urgent and safe cases, renters and car owners, older and younger people. All fictional.

## Sunday morning
4. **Phone mockups** for the pitch: the iMessage thread and the incoming call (Figma frame 6).
5. **Devpost draft** in `docs/DEVPOST.md` (Dev B or the owner can commit it): inspiration, what it does, how we built it, challenges, what's next. Due 10am.
6. **Demo video storyboard** (2 min max, demo only), following the spec's demo script. Then rehearse playing the victim on stage.
